# Script Outline

This document contains the situations I want to create, regardless
of the actual wordings given.

## Opening

### First Room
You are given the choice of becoming one of the four major classes.
* A Hindu
* An Ahmedi
* A Shi'a
* A Christian

### Second Room
This is the opening. Open with a flourish, and some news post about
a bomb blast and the state of Pakistan.

This will have at least one class-specific line.

### Third Room

This is where we go our different ways. Each class does it's own
thing. We go through a series of rooms telling what we know about
the person. This room focuses on professional life.

* **Hindu**:            The hindu is a rominent businessman in sindh.
* **Ahmedi**:           The Ahmedi is a physicist in a local university in punjab.
* **Shi'a**:            Shi'a is a doctor in Balochistan.
* **Christian**:        

### Fourth Room

This is where we write about the guy's family. The choices here were
limited both by the profession and the treatment of the minority
itself.

* **Hindu**:            Is married. Has a family with two daughters and a son.
* **Ahmedi**:           Is engaged to be married.
* **Shi'a**:            Is an only child.
* **Christian**:        To Be Decided.

### Fifth Room

This is where we show a simple act of discrimination against the
specific minority. We let the person act out, or suffer in silence.
Regardless of the choice they make here, the major act of violence
will happen. But if they act out, we immediately skip to Seventh
Room. If they decide to suffer in silence, we  make them go through
one additional room, Sixth Room.

* **Hindu**:            Has a contract cancelled because of him being a hindu. He can either *go and complain to the contractor* OR *let this incident go as this isn't the first time this has happened*.
* **Ahmedi**:           Is refused service at a medical store, for being an Ahmedi. He can either *demand that he be allowed the medicine forcefully* OR *let this incident go as this isn't the first time this has happened.*
* **Shi'a**:            Is refused promotion on account of being a shi'a. He can either *Write an application, indicating the nepotism and discrimination* OR *let this incident go as this isn't the first time this has happened.*
* **Christian**:        To Be Decided

### Sixth Room

This is where things seem to go wrong. The player hears rumors. And
generally becomes terrified of the state of affairs. This is also
where we can possibly have a person from other minority. I'm not
sure. This is a fairly general room, and I guess we can have some
generic threats and the information about the deteriorating
situation here.

### Seventh Room

This is the climactic scene. This is where tragedy strikes. The
number of rooms after this is not important and dependant on the
minority class.

* **Hindu**:            The daughter gets kidnapped and raped and converted. This can possibly stretch to a few more rooms. I don't really care.
* **Ahmedi**:           He gets fired, his assets get frozen, he gets charged with a crime he didn't commit and ultimately commits suicide.
* **Shi'a**:            He gets killed.
* **Christian**:        He gets accused of blasphemy. His whole house along with his family burns down.
